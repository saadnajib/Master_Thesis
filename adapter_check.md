## Camera convention

For each frame, `jointPositions[person]` reshaped to `[24,3]` is treated as world-space and transformed as:

```text
X_cam = R @ X_world + t
```

where `R=cam_poses[k][:3,:3]` and `t=cam_poses[k][:3,3]`. This convention is decisively supported by projection against the 13 corresponding OpenPose BODY-18 points (confidence > 0): raw 3DPW joints project with mean **8.88 px** (median 7.01, p95 22.15, max 28.33). The inverse camera-pose interpretation has approximately 2400 px mean error and negative/inappropriate depth. The pilot's source code (`threedpw.py`) uses the same `R @ ... + t` convention.

## Adapter transform and numerical checks

For each pilot frame, the implemented transform is exactly:

```text
root_trans = R @ (fit_transl + trans_src) + t
Qcam[0]    = R @ Qfit[0]
Qcam[1:]   = Qfit[1:]
```

The Anny matrices are identity 4x4 matrices with these rotations, with translation only in matrix 0. The pilot `anny_joints` include `fit_transl`; for geometry comparisons I used:

```text
anny_joints_cam = R @ anny_joints_fit + R @ trans_src + t
smpl_joints_cam = R @ (smpl_joints_npz + trans_src) + t
```

The Anny-vs-fitted-SMPL check (same target used by the fitting job) gives:

- all 24 joints: mean **40.85 mm**, median 12.81 mm, p95 163.51 mm;
- reliable limb set from `smpl_to_anny.py`: mean **13.01 mm**, median 10.22 mm, p95 49.18 mm, max 52.71 mm.

Thus the stated approximately 15 mm reliable-limb fit quality is preserved after the rigid camera transform. The all-joint value remains dominated by the known torso/hip/collar definition mismatch.

## Independent raw 3DPW joint check and pelvis-origin diagnosis

The independent camera-space joints from raw `jointPositions` are valid for projection, but are not numerically identical to the neutral-SMPL joints saved in the pilot fit:

- transformed pilot SMPL vs raw 3DPW joints: all-joint mean **57.85 mm**, reliable mean **72.86 mm**;
- transformed Anny vs raw 3DPW joints: all-joint mean **87.38 mm**, reliable mean **74.91 mm**;
- Anny root vs raw 3DPW pelvis discrepancy: **32.41 mm** mean (32.56 median, 32.96 p95, 33.00 max), effectively a fixed offset.

The fixed source-frame pelvis offsets explain this rather than a camera convention error:

```text
raw jointPositions[0] - trans       = [-1.028, -234.516, 32.762] mm
pilot smpl_joints[0] (transl=0)     = [-1.564, -202.803, 26.720] mm
raw minus pilot pelvis origin       = [ 0.536,  -31.713,   6.042] mm
```

The source 3DPW `jointPositions` and the neutral SMPL forward model used in the pilot therefore use pelvis origins differing by a fixed ~32 mm vector. This is the same class of origin correction noted in `threedpw.py` (`-root_cam`). It does **not** invalidate the Anny formula: the pilot Anny root is at the model origin to numerical precision, so no additional `-root_cam` term is applied to `root_trans`. The pilot-fit target comparison is the appropriate check for the adapter itself.

Projection of the 13 mapped pilot points (confidence > 0) over 60 frames:

- raw 3DPW joints: **8.88 px** mean;
- transformed pilot SMPL joints: **18.13 px** mean;
- transformed Anny fit joints: **23.08 px** mean (reliable mapped subset 20.28 px).

These pixel residuals are consistent with the independent raw-joint 2D tracking residual and the known fit/model correspondence mismatch; they do not indicate a wrong camera transform.
